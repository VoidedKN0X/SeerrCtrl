<p>Discover, request and manage movies and series on your self-hosted Seerr, Jellyseerr or Overseerr server.</p>
<ul>
<li>Sign in with Jellyfin/Emby (Quick Connect), Plex or a Seerr account</li>
<li>Discover with the web UI's sliders and filters, plus search</li>
<li>Title pages with ratings, cast, collections and recommendations</li>
<li>Request movies and series, in 4K too, with season picking and advanced options</li>
<li>Approve or decline requests with a swipe</li>
<li>Notifications for your requests and issues, with Approve/Decline for managers</li>
<li>Upcoming calendar, Watchlist and Profile</li>
<li>Report, comment on and resolve issues</li>
<li>Full admin: users, permissions, quotas, blocklist, Radarr/Sonarr, jobs and settings</li>
<li>App lock with PIN and fingerprint; tablet and foldable layout</li>
<li>Private by default: hidden from screenshots, no Gravatar or Plex pictures unless you turn them on</li>
</ul>
<p>Unofficial app, not affiliated with the Seerr, Jellyseerr or Overseerr projects.</p>
<p>Google-free and de-Googled: no Google Play services, Firebase or other Google services, and the app never contacts Google. It works the same on de-Googled phones (GrapheneOS, CalyxOS, /e/OS, LineageOS without Google apps).</p>
<p>Telemetry-free: no analytics, crash reporting, usage stats or tracking of any kind.</p>
